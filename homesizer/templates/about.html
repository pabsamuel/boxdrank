How the calculators are built

Every calculator on this site shows its formula on the same page as its answer, under "How this is calculated". The inputs come from published standards and manufacturer sizing practice — AHAM for dehumidifier and air purifier capacity, DOE first-hour-rating figures for water heaters, and the ceiling-height-over-two convention for downlight spacing. Where a rule of thumb stops being good enough, the page says so and says what to do instead.

What this site is not

It is not a substitute for a Manual J load calculation, a licensed electrician, or your local code. Sizing is the part you can do at the kitchen table; installation generally is not.

How it makes money

Display advertising, and affiliate commission on some product links — marked as such, and they never change which product the table recommends or what the calculator outputs. The spec tables carry the date they were last checked, because a price table without a date is a price table nobody maintains.
