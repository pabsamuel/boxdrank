Template Guard — Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

What we store

  • Board configuration of the boards you mark as templates and the boards you compare against them: board and column IDs, column titles, types and settings, groups, views, owners and subscribers as user IDs, and (where enabled) automation recipes.
  • The monday user ID of the person who installed the app, so drift alerts have somewhere to go.
  • An OAuth access token for your account, encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM.
  • Your plan and alert settings, if you change them.

What we never store

Your item data — no item names, no column values, no updates, no files. The app requests no item, update or file permissions, and no write permission, so it cannot read or change them. This is also enforced in code: every write to storage is checked and refused if it contains item data.

Where it lives

On monday.com's own infrastructure (monday code, EU region). We do not use third-party servers, analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.

Retention and deletion

Everything we hold for your account is deleted when you uninstall Template Guard. You can also remove a saved template at any time from inside the app.
