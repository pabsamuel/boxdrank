## 1. What the app does

permissions and never posts, changes or deletes an update, an item or a board.

## 2. What it does not promise

The app shows what monday.com reports, and applies one rule: an update counts
as answered when someone other than its author replied to it, or posted a newer
update on the same item. It cannot:
- show updates on boards you do not have access to;
- know whether a question was answered somewhere else, such as by email, in a
  meeting, or on another item;
- read further back than 30 days, or more updates than the page says it read;
