Why it isn't robotic

We don't re-read the line. We change the voice.

Most AI voice-over reads the script from scratch — which is why it comes out flat and robotic: the actor's choices are gone before the model starts. We take the game's original recording and change only who is speaking.

Text-to-speech

The model rebuilds the line from text. It guesses where to shout and where to pause — and usually guesses wrong. The length changes too, so lip-sync drifts.

Voice conversion

The actor's real performance is kept; only the timbre becomes yours. The length stays identical, so lip-sync holds and the emotion survives.

Consent

A voice belongs to its owner. We enforce that in code.

The biggest scandal around AI voices in games has been voice actors copied without permission. We only work with people who hand over their own voice.

Your own voice

You agree to the consent form yourself. Nobody else can send in your voice for you.

Someone else's voice

Your partner or friend records their own confirmation and gives their own email. You can't agree on their behalf.

Withdraw any time

Withdraw and the cloned voice, and everything made from it, is destroyed. Files already delivered can't be recalled — we say so up front.

Your voice never goes to an AI company

Conversion runs on our own machine. Your recording is never uploaded to a third-party AI service.

We don't clone celebrities, streamers, politicians or professional voice actors — at any price.

Questions

What people ask.

Which games does it work with?

Games that store voice lines as plain .wav or .ogg files are the easiest — you can swap the files directly. For games that pack their audio (.pak, .bnk, .bank) we hand you ready-made audio files and install notes for that engine. Before you pay, we look at your game and tell you how well it fits.

Can you change the language?

It works in whatever language the game is in, with your voice — that is what sounds most natural. Changing the language is a different job that we don't offer yet, because it means reading the line from scratch, and that is exactly what makes it robotic.

Can I use it in online games?

We don't recommend it. Multiplayer games can treat modified game files as cheating and restrict your account. This is for single-player games.

Is it legal?

It's a personal voice mod, for a game you own, for your own use. We deliver it on the condition that you don't share, upload or sell the pack. We never send you the game itself — only lines voiced as you.

How much recording do you need?

At least 30 seconds, ideally 90, of clean speech. The recording page asks for eight short lines and checks each one instantly; a quiet room and your phone's microphone are enough.

What if I don't like it?

You pay after hearing the free sample. Paid orders include one round of fixes — if a line came out badly, we redo just that line.
