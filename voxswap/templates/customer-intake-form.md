# VoxSwap order form

Copy this into an email or a form and send it to the customer. Everything here
maps to a field in `order.json`, so a complete answer means a job you can start
the same day.

## 1. You

- Your name:
- Your email:
- What language do you want us to reply in?

## 2. What are we re-voicing?

- Game or film?
- Exact title and version/platform (e.g. "Cyberpunk 2077, PC, GOG", "Blade Runner 2049, my own Blu-ray rip"):
- Which character(s)? Name them exactly as they appear in the game/credits.
- Do you want the dialogue in a different language than the original? Which one?

## 3. Whose voice goes in?

For **each** person (you, your partner, a friend — anyone):

- Their name:
- Their own email address (we need to contact them directly, not through you):
- Which character do they play?
- Are they the person filling in this form? yes / no

> Every voice needs that person's own signed consent and a short verification
> recording. We cannot start without it, and we cannot accept consent passed on
> by someone else. This is not negotiable, and it is what makes us safe to use.

## 4. Recordings

Send us, per person:

- **At least 90 seconds** of clean speech. Two to five minutes is much better.
- Quiet room, no music, no other voices, no headphones mic if you can avoid it.
- Varied delivery: normal talking, a question, something excited, something quiet.
- WAV or FLAC if you can; MP3/M4A is fine.
- Plus the verification phrase we will send you (one sentence, ~10 seconds).

## 5. The files we work from

- How will you send the game audio / film file? (link, drive, etc.)
- Do you have a script, subtitle file (.srt) or dialogue list? Send it — it
  improves quality and lowers the price.

## 6. Delivery

- What do you want back? (replacement audio files / a modded folder / a film with an extra audio track)
- Deadline, if any:

---

**What you get back:** a ZIP with the audio, a `manifest.json` listing every
file it replaces (with checksums, so you can restore), install instructions for
your specific title, and a quality report.

**What we do not do:** voices of celebrities, voice actors, streamers or public
figures without their own signed consent; anything intended to make someone
appear to say something they did not agree to.
