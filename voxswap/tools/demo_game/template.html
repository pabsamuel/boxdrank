Station Four is a game build in miniature: eight voiced lines shipped with one actor, then run through the same pipeline a paying order goes through, with a customer's voice in the actor's place. Switch voices mid-scene and play the line again.

Nothing was re-read for the second take. The original recording was converted, so the timing, the pauses and the shouting are the first actor's performance in someone else's voice — which is why both takes are the same length to the millisecond.

Both voices are synthetic stand-ins, not real people: the “actor” and the “customer” are two speakers from the LibriTTS corpus (CC BY 4.0), voiced with Piper and converted with FreeVC. The game, its script and its scenes were written for this demo.
