# The offer

The posts are marketing. **This file is the business.** Get this wrong and 100k followers earn
you nothing — which is the normal outcome for language accounts.

---

## The math that decides the product

Your original idea was subscribers practicing in your DMs. Here is what that costs:

| Subscribers | Realistic DM load @ 4 min each, daily | Your day |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | 40 min | fine |
| 50 | 3 h 20 min | already over budget |
| 100 | 6 h 40 min | **full-time job at ~$1,000/month** |
| 250 | 16 h | impossible |

Unlimited 1:1 support is a business that **punishes you for succeeding**. Every tier below is
built so that your time cost grows far slower than revenue.

## Tiers

### Free — the audience
Daily post + weekly "Broadcast Channel" drill. Costs you nothing extra; it's the same content.
Purpose: build the list and prove you're worth paying.

### Tier 1 — **The Drill** · $9/month · unlimited seats
- Members-only weekly PDF drill pack (compiled from your own posts — zero extra work)
- Full ebook library access, one new ebook per quarter
- Members-only Broadcast Channel where you answer *group* questions, not individual ones

**Your time cost: ~30 min/week, flat, regardless of subscriber count.** This is the tier that
scales. Most of your revenue will come from here.

### Tier 2 — **The Room** · $39/month · capped at 40 seats
- Everything in Tier 1
- One 45-minute group speaking call per week (Zoom, recorded, 8–12 people talking)
- Async voice-note feedback: send up to **3 voice notes per week**, you reply within 24h
  on weekdays with a corrected voice note

**Your time cost: ~4 h/week at a full 40 seats.** Revenue at cap: **$1,560/month.**
The cap is the product — "40 seats" is what makes it sell, and what stops it eating you alive.

### Tier 3 — **1:1** · $150/month · capped at 6 seats
Weekly 30-minute private call + unlimited async voice notes. ~5 h/week at cap, $900/month.
Open this only when Tier 2 has a waitlist. It is a premium anchor that makes $39 look cheap.

### Realistic target at month 12
120 × $9 + 25 × $39 + 3 × $150 = **$2,505/month** for roughly 12 h/week. That is a good
part-time business and an honest expectation.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Every support promise has a number and a window.** "3 voice notes/week, 24h reply,
   weekdays." Never "unlimited," never "anytime." Unlimited promises are how creators quit.
2. **Caps are advertised, not hidden.** "11 of 40 seats left" is your best converter and it is
   also simply true.
3. **Never discount. Ever.** Discounting a subscription trains the audience to wait and it
   insults the people already paying. Add value instead.
4. **Annual plan at 10 months' price** from day one. It fixes cash flow and churn simultaneously.
5. **Cancel in one click, no retention interrogation.** It is required in most markets and it is
   also the only way to get honest churn data.

## The waitlist launch (do this, not a slow public launch)

Do not add a "subscribe" link the day you hit some follower number. Instead:

1. **From day 1**, every post ends with the same CTA to a free email/Broadcast list.
2. At ~1,500 followers, open a **waitlist**, not a product. "40 seats, opening in March."
3. Two weeks before launch, ask the waitlist one question: *"What happens in the moment you
   freeze in English?"* Their replies become the sales page, verbatim.
4. Open for **5 days only**, then close. Closing is what creates the decision.
5. Re-open quarterly. Between opens, the waitlist grows and the scarcity is real.

A 40-seat launch to a 300-person engaged waitlist is very achievable. A "subscribe" button on a
cold 10k-follower page typically converts under 0.5%.
