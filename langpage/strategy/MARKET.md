# Market selection

## The question we are actually answering

Not "which language has the most learners" — that is Spanish and it is the wrong answer.
The question is:

> Which language pair maximizes **(paying subscribers) × (price they tolerate)** for a solo
> creator starting from zero, on Instagram, in 2026?

Volume is the *least* important input. A page with 8,000 highly-targeted Gulf professionals
out-earns a page with 80,000 casual LatAm learners, every time.

---

## Scoring

Weights reflect what actually drives subscription revenue:

| Factor | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Willingness to pay (USD) | 30% | The whole business is USD ARPU. A 10× currency gap cannot be out-hustled. |
| Competition (inverted) | 25% | A saturated niche means zero organic reach for a new account. This is the silent killer. |
| Audience volume | 20% | Matters, but only after the three above. |
| Payment rails | 15% | Can they actually check out? Card penetration, currency, refund friction. |
| Content feasibility | 10% | Can *you* produce credible daily content in that explainer language at 45 min/day? |

## Why #1 wins: English → Arabic, Gulf-targeted

- **The payer profile is a working adult, not a student.** A 29-year-old Saudi or Emirati
  professional needs English for promotion, not for fun. Career-driven learners convert at
  multiples of hobby learners and churn far less.
- **Disposable income in USD is genuinely high** in SA/UAE/KW/QA, and the region has extremely
  high Instagram penetration.
- **Structural tailwind:** Saudi Arabia is the Middle East's largest English-learning market,
  driven by Vision 2030 workforce-English policy
  ([Cognitive Market Research](https://www.cognitivemarketresearch.com/english-language-learning-market-report)).
  You are selling into a demand curve the government is pushing up.
- **The niche is thin on Instagram.** English-taught-in-Arabic with a *premium professional*
  positioning is underserved compared to the thousands of Spanish-language and generic
  "learn English" accounts. This is where the 25% competition weight pays out.

### What kills the runner-ups

- **Japanese** pays the best of anyone, but the content bar is brutal (register, keigo, and a
  learner culture with high expectations of polish), and the audience's habits lean LINE and
  YouTube over Instagram DMs.
- **Spanish** is the trap everybody falls into. Enormous volume, near-zero USD ARPU, and the
  most saturated corner of language Instagram on earth. A new account gets no distribution.
- **Turkish** is the comfortable choice and the wrong one: lira-denominated ARPU means you need
  roughly 5–8× the subscribers for the same revenue.

### The one worth a second account later

**German → Arabic/Turkish, aimed at Ausbildung and nursing-visa candidates.** Small audience,
but the highest-intent buyers in all of language learning — the outcome they're paying for is a
residence permit. Price tolerance is extreme. Revisit at month 6, not before.

## Positioning (this is the part that actually matters)

Do **not** launch "Learn English." That is invisible.

Launch: **"English for Gulf professionals who already understand it but freeze when they have
to speak."**

That sentence does four jobs at once — it names the person, names the pain (freezing, not
grammar), excludes beginners (who don't pay), and implies the product (speaking practice, which
is exactly what the paid tier sells).

**Target avatar:** 25–38, works at a bank / Aramco / a ministry / a startup in Riyadh, Jeddah,
Dubai or Abu Dhabi. Reads English fine. Sat through 12 years of English class. Still panics in
an English meeting with the regional director. Has money. Has no time. Is embarrassed.

Every post must be aimed at *that specific person*. If a post would also work for a bored
teenager in any country, it is too generic — rewrite it.

## How to know this call was wrong

Re-open this file at **day 45** and check against `ops/METRICS.md`:

- Saves-per-post under 2% of reach, three weeks running → the pain point is wrong, not the market.
- Audience insights show under 50% of followers in Gulf countries → targeting leaked; fix hooks
  and hashtags before touching the market choice.
- Strong reach, but the waitlist won't convert at any price → the *offer* is wrong. Go to
  `strategy/OFFER.md`, not back to this file.

Change the market at most once. Two pivots is just quitting with extra steps.
