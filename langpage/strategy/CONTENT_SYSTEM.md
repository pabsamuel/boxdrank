# The content system

The goal is not "post every day." The goal is **post every day for 18 months without deciding
what to post.** Decision fatigue is what ends these projects, not lack of ideas.

So: five fixed formats, one per weekday. You never choose a format again — only the content
inside it.

---

## The five formats

| Day | Format | What it does | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | **The Freeze** | One real situation where a Gulf professional freezes in English, and the exact phrase that unfreezes it. | 10 min |
| Tue | **Wrong / Right** | A mistake Arabic speakers make (often L1 interference), the correction, and *why* — in Arabic. | 8 min |
| Wed | **Carousel: 5 ways to say X** | Ranked by formality: casual → neutral → boardroom. This is the highest-save format. | 20 min |
| Thu | **Reel: 30-second shadowing drill** | You say a phrase 3× at increasing speed; they repeat. The highest-reach format. | 25 min |
| Fri | **Sounds Senior** | A phrase that makes someone sound junior, and the senior version. Directly on-avatar. | 10 min |

Weekend: **off.** Scheduled rest is part of the system, not a failure of it. Burnout at week 6
is the single most likely way this ends.

---

## Batch, don't drip

Never create on the day you post. Once a week:

- **Sunday, 2.5 hours:** run `prompts/weekly-batch.md` → 7 posts drafted, reviewed, scheduled.
- **Every weekday, 15 minutes:** reply to every comment and DM within the first 60 minutes after
  posting. **This is not optional** — early engagement drives distribution, and DM conversations
  are where your paying subscribers come from.

That's ~2.5 h + 75 min/week ≈ 45 min/day averaged. Exactly the budget.

---

## Rules for every single post

1. **Arabic for the explanation, English for the target language.** Always both. The Arabic is
   the reason they follow *you* instead of a native-speaker account.
2. **One idea per post.** Five phrases is one idea ("ways to say X"). Five unrelated tips is not.
3. **The hook must name the person or the moment**, in the first 3 words.
   Bad: "5 useful English phrases." Good: "Your manager just said 'circle back.'"
4. **Every post ends with the same CTA.** One CTA, forever, changing only at launch windows.
5. **Audio on everything possible.** Pronunciation is the thing text-based competitors cannot
   deliver, and it's what the paid tier sells.
6. **Never teach beginner content.** Beginners don't pay and they attract more beginners. If a
   post would help someone at A1, cut it.

## The archive — do this from post #1

Every published post gets logged in `templates/calendar.csv` **with its actual copy**. This file
is not admin busywork; it is:

- the raw material your quarterly ebooks are compiled from (`prompts/ebook-compile.md`)
- the input to your monthly performance review (`prompts/monthly-review.md`)
- your defense against repeating yourself in month 9

Thirty seconds after each post. If you skip this, you lose the ebook product entirely and you
will be writing them from scratch at 3am instead.

## Hashtags and reach

Three to five hashtags, Arabic and English mixed, all narrow and on-avatar
(`#انجليزي_للعمل`-type tags, not `#english`). Broad tags put you in front of the wrong
audience, which trains the algorithm to show you to the wrong audience permanently.

Geographic signal matters: reference Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Abu Dhabi by name in captions where
it's natural. It sharpens targeting far more than hashtags do.
