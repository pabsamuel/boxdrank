Analyze in this order and be blunt — I want to know what isn't working:

2. **Targeting leak.** Is the audience actually Gulf professionals? If under 50% of followers are

3. **Saves vs reach.** Saves are the signal that matters (see `ops/METRICS.md`). Which topics get

4. **The DM questions.** The 3 most common questions are free market research. What product or
   post series do they point at?
5. **One thing to stop.** Name the single lowest-return activity and tell me to stop doing it.
6. **One experiment for next month.** Exactly one, with a number attached that defines success.

Do **not** tell me to post more, use trending audio, or be more consistent unless the data
specifically shows that. Check my numbers against `ops/METRICS.md` kill criteria and tell me
plainly if I've hit any of them.
