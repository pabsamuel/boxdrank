# PROMPT: Compile the quarterly ebook

This is the prompt that turns 60 posts you already wrote into a product you can sell, with no
new writing. Run once a quarter, ~1 hour.

**Original content only.** Everything in the ebook comes from your own archive. Nothing found,
scanned, or copied from a textbook.

---

Read `langpage/templates/calendar.csv` — all rows from the last 90 days — plus
`langpage/CLAUDE.md` and `langpage/templates/brand.md`.

Compile a sellable ebook from this archive.

**Step 1 — Find the spine.** Cluster the 90 days of posts into 4–6 themes. Tell me which theme
had the highest average saves; that theme becomes the ebook's title and organizing principle.
Propose 3 title options in the style of "50 English Phrases That Make You Sound Senior in a
Meeting." Wait for me to choose.

**Step 2 — Structure.** Then produce a full outline:
- 5–7 chapters, each a cluster of related posts
- each chapter: 8–12 phrases, ordered casual → boardroom
- each phrase: English, Arabic explanation, one workplace example sentence, and a note on when
  *not* to use it (this last part is what separates it from a free list)

**Step 3 — Add what a PDF can do that a post can't:**
- a 10-question self-check at the end of each chapter, with an answer key
- one "drill" per chapter: a 5-minute speaking exercise they do alone
- a one-page cheat sheet at the end, designed to be printed

**Step 4 — Write it.** Chapter by chapter, stopping after each so I can verify the Arabic.

**Step 5 — Front matter.** A one-page intro in Arabic saying who this is for and who it isn't,
plus a page pointing to the paid tier.

Constraints:
- Nothing beginner-level.
- Every example is workplace context, culturally appropriate for Gulf readers.
- Flag every Arabic sentence you are less than fully confident in.
- If the archive doesn't have enough material for a chapter, say so instead of padding it with
  generic content. A thin honest ebook beats a padded one.
