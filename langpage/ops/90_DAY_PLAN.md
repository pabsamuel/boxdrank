# The first 90 days

One rule above all others: **you do not get to build anything new until you have posted 30 days
straight.** Not a website, not a logo redesign, not a second account. Posting is the only thing
that compounds.

---

## Week 0 — Setup (one afternoon, 4 hours, once)

- [ ] Fill in `templates/brand.md`. 20 minutes. Don't perfect it.
- [ ] Create the account. Professional/Creator type. Handle should say what you do.
- [ ] Bio: who it's for + what they get + the CTA. Written for the Riyadh banker, nobody else.
- [ ] Set up the free email list (Kit) and the Instagram Broadcast Channel.
- [ ] Create a Canva template per format so visuals are never a decision again.
- [ ] Turn on 2FA with an authenticator app.
- [ ] Run `prompts/weekly-batch.md` for week 1.

Do **not** set up payments yet. You have nothing to sell.

## Weeks 1–4 — Prove you won't quit

Goal: **20 posts published.** Follower count is explicitly not a goal this month.

- Post Mon–Fri, rest on weekends.
- 15 minutes of replies right after each post.
- Log every post in `templates/calendar.csv` the same day.
- Sunday: batch next week.
- Day 30: run `prompts/monthly-review.md`.

Expected: 100–400 followers. **This is normal.** Most accounts look dead for 6 weeks and then
one post does 50k. You cannot get that post without the 30 boring ones first.

## Weeks 5–8 — Find what works

Goal: **double down on the format that earns its time.**

- Cut or reduce the lowest-performing format if the month-1 review says so.
- Start the free weekly drill in the Broadcast Channel (this is Tier 1's product, being
  tested for free).
- Start DM conversations deliberately: reply to every commenter with a real question.
- At 1,500 followers: **open the waitlist.** Not the product — the waitlist.
- Day 60: monthly review.

Expected: 500–2,000 followers, 50–200 on the list.

## Weeks 9–12 — First launch

Goal: **first paying subscribers, however few.**

- Set up Lemon Squeezy/Gumroad (`strategy/MONEY.md`). One afternoon.
- Ask the waitlist the freeze question. Collect verbatim answers.
- Run `prompts/dm-and-sales.md` Part B with those answers.
- **5-day launch window**, then close.
- Day 90: monthly review + compile the first ebook from your archive
  (`prompts/ebook-compile.md`).

Expected: **8–25 paying subscribers.** If you get 8, that is a success, not a failure. It means
the machine works and now you only have to feed it.

---

## Kill criteria — decide these now, while calm

Written in advance so you judge by evidence, not by how you feel on a bad Tuesday.

| Checkpoint | If this is true | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Day 30 | Fewer than 20 posts published | The problem is the routine, not the strategy. Cut to 3 posts/week and continue. Do not pivot. |
| Day 45 | Under 50% of followers in Gulf countries | Targeting leak. Fix hooks and geography signals. Do not change market. |
| Day 60 | Saves under 2% of reach, three weeks running | Wrong pain point. Interview 5 followers before changing anything. |
| Day 90 | Waitlist over 300 but fewer than 8 subscribers | Offer problem. Rework `OFFER.md`. Do not post more. |
| Day 90 | Fewer than 300 followers **and** you dread posting | Stop. This one is honest advice: a business you hate at month 3 does not survive month 18. |

**What is not a kill criterion:** a slow first month, a post that flops, a rude comment, or a
competitor with more followers. All four are guaranteed and none of them mean anything.
