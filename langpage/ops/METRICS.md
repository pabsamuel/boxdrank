# Metrics

## The six numbers that matter

| # | Metric | Why | Healthy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Saves ÷ reach** | The single best predictor of whether someone would pay. A save means "this is useful to *me*." | 2–5% |
| 2 | **Shares ÷ reach** | Shares are how you reach new people. Saves prove value, shares create growth. | 1–3% |
| 3 | **Profile visits ÷ reach** | Did the post make them curious about *you*, or just entertain them? | 3–8% |
| 4 | **List signups per week** | The only asset you own outright. | 15+/wk by day 60 |
| 5 | **% followers in SA/UAE/KW/QA** | Targeting integrity. Everything downstream depends on this. | 60%+ |
| 6 | **Real DM conversations per week** | Not message count — actual back-and-forth. These become subscribers. | 5+/wk by day 45 |

## The numbers to deliberately ignore

- **Follower count.** 10,000 wrong followers is worth less than 1,000 right ones, and chasing it
  makes you post generic content. Look at it monthly, never daily.
- **Likes.** Cheapest signal there is. Costs nothing to give, predicts nothing.
- **Views on a viral reel.** A reel that reaches 200k of the wrong people damages your targeting
  for weeks afterward. A viral post is only good if metrics 1, 3 and 5 hold up on it.
- **Anyone else's growth.** You cannot see their churn, their hours, or their ad spend.

## Post-level review, 30 seconds

For every post, after 72 hours, log reach / saves / shares / profile visits in
`templates/calendar.csv`. That's it. The analysis happens monthly, not daily. Checking Insights
hourly is a way of feeling productive while producing nothing.

## The one leading indicator

**Posts published per week.** It is the only metric fully under your control, and at this stage
it predicts everything else. If that number holds at 5 for 12 weeks, the rest follows.
