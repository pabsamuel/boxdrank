## 1. What the app does

The app reads your boards' activity through monday.com's API, learns how often
each automation normally acts, and tells you when one that used to act
regularly goes quiet — on a board view, and by email to the person who set up
alerts. It asks for read-only permissions and never changes a board, an item
or an automation.

## 2. What it does not promise

The app looks for automations that **stop**. It cannot:

- see an automation that acts too rarely to have a rhythm, or one that has
  never acted;
- tell an automation switched off on purpose from one that broke, except by
  how long it has been quiet;
- guarantee that every stopped automation is noticed, or noticed in time.

It is a safety net, not a replacement for checking automations that matter to
you. **We are not responsible for work that did or did not happen because an
automation stopped, whether or not the app reported it.**
